Step 1 · Before you build anything

The Stress Audit worksheet

For one week, write down every task you do more than once. Then score each one. The task with the highest total is your first agent's job.

TaskHours a weekDrains me? (0–3)Repeats the same way? (0–3)Needs me personally? (3 = no, 0 = yes)Total
Example: answering price questions in DMs43339

Then map that one job

  1. What starts it? A new DM, a form, a Monday morning.
  2. What do you do, step by step? Write it like you're training a new assistant.
  3. What does "done" look like? Booked, answered, list delivered.
  4. When should it stop and get you? An upset customer, a custom quote, a big order.

That page becomes your agent's instructions. You can't hand off a job you haven't written down.

Service businesses · Lead Finder

Lead Finder

Finds people and businesses who need what you sell, and hands you a short list every week.

ChatGPT or Claude (with web search)Google SheetWeekly reminder or scheduled task

Set it up

  1. Describe your ideal customer in 3 lines: who they are, where they are, and what tells you they need you.
  2. Create a Google Sheet with columns: Name, Where found, Why they fit, Contact, Status.
  3. Paste the starter prompt below into your AI, filled in with your details.
  4. Set it to run every Monday, as a scheduled task if your AI tool has one, or a calendar reminder to paste it in.
  5. Add the best leads to your sheet. You approve every message before it goes out.
You are my Lead Finder for [business name], a [type of business] in [city/area]. My ideal customer: [3 lines from step 1]. Search the web for 10 people or businesses posted in the last 7 days who match. Look at [local Facebook groups, Google Maps, new business listings, Nextdoor, event pages]. For each one give me: name, where you found them, why they fit, and how to contact them. Then write a short, friendly first message for each in my voice: warm, plain, no pressure. Do not send anything. I review everything first.

Weekly check: Did at least 3 of the 10 actually fit? If not, tighten the ideal customer lines.

Service businesses · Lead Follow-Up

Lead Follow-Up Agent

Makes sure nobody who asked about your service gets forgotten.

Google Sheet or your CRMChatGPT or ClaudeEmail tool like MailerLite

Set it up

  1. List everyone who asked in the last 30 days and didn't book: name, what they asked about, date, how they reached you.
  2. Decide your rhythm: day 1, day 3, day 7, then once a month.
  3. Use the starter prompt to write the follow-ups for each step, in your voice.
  4. Put the emails into an automation, or keep the texts saved and send from your sheet each morning.
  5. Anyone who replies goes straight to you.
You are my Lead Follow-Up assistant for [business name]. Here is someone who asked about [service] on [date] and didn't book: [paste their message]. Write 3 follow-up texts I can send on day 1, day 3 and day 7. Keep each under 40 words, warm and plain, no pressure, no fake urgency. Each one should give them a reason to reply (a question, a helpful tip or an open date), and the last one should make it easy to say "not right now."

Weekly check: How many people replied, and how many booked? Rewrite the message that gets the fewest replies.

Service businesses · DM-to-Booking

DM-to-Booking Responder

Answers the same questions about prices, availability and booking in your voice, and moves people to book.

ManyChat or your inbox's saved repliesChatGPT or ClaudeYour booking link

Set it up

  1. Write down your top 10 questions and your real answers, the way you'd say them.
  2. Use the starter prompt to turn them into short replies that end with a next step.
  3. Load them into ManyChat as keyword replies, or as saved replies in your inbox.
  4. Every reply ends with your booking link or one simple question.
  5. Set a rule: complaints, custom quotes and anything unusual come to you.
You are my booking assistant for [business name]. Here are my top questions and my real answers: [paste your list]. Rewrite each answer as a reply under 50 words, in a warm, plain voice. End every reply with one next step: my booking link [link] or one simple question. Then list the kinds of messages you should NOT answer and should send to me instead.

Weekly check: Read 5 real conversations. Fix any reply that sounded robotic or missed the question.

TikTok and online sellers · Audience Scout

Audience Scout

Finds the trends, hooks and buyers in your niche and hands you a weekly content and live plan.

ChatGPT or Claude (with web search)TikTok search and Creative CenterNotes or Google Doc

Set it up

  1. Write your niche, your product and who buys it in 3 lines.
  2. Each week, save 10 videos in your niche that did well, and paste the captions or hooks into a doc.
  3. Run the starter prompt with your notes.
  4. Pick 3 videos and 1 live topic from the plan. Film in a batch.
You are my Audience Scout. My niche: [niche]. My product: [product]. My buyer: [who buys]. Here are hooks and captions from videos doing well in my niche this week: [paste]. Tell me what these have in common, then give me 7 video ideas and 1 live topic for next week. For each video: the hook (first line), the point, and the call to action that leads to my product. Keep it honest: no fake results or income claims.

Weekly check: Which idea got the most saves and comments? Tell your scout next week so it learns.

TikTok and online sellers · Buyer Follow-Up

Buyer Follow-Up Agent

Keeps talking to people after the video or live ends, so sales don't slip away.

ManyChatEmail tool like MailerLiteChatGPT or Claude

Set it up

  1. Give people a reason to join your list: a free guide, a checklist, or a live replay.
  2. Use a comment keyword in ManyChat to send the link and collect their email.
  3. Use the starter prompt to write a 3-email follow-up.
  4. Load it into an email automation that starts when someone joins.
You are my Buyer Follow-Up writer. I sell [product] for [price] to [buyer]. Someone just asked for my free [lead magnet] after a TikTok video or live. Write 3 short emails: day 0 (deliver the freebie and one quick win), day 2 (a real story or result and what it took), day 4 (an invitation to buy, with an easy no). Warm, plain, no pressure, no countdown tricks. Each email under 150 words.

Weekly check: Look at opens and clicks for each email. Rewrite the subject line of the weakest one.

TikTok and online sellers · Comment-to-Sale

Comment-to-Sale Responder

Answers "link?" and "how much?" in your voice and sends the right product link the moment someone is ready.

Good to know: ManyChat keyword replies work on Instagram and Facebook. TikTok doesn't allow them, so on TikTok, use saved replies that point to the link in your bio.

ManyChatChatGPT or ClaudeYour product links

Set it up

  1. Pick one keyword per product, like GUIDE or KIT.
  2. Use the starter prompt to write the reply for each keyword.
  3. Set each one up in ManyChat as a comment keyword that sends a DM with the link.
  4. Say the keyword in every video and live: "Comment KIT and I'll send it to you."
You are my Comment-to-Sale assistant. My products: [list each product, price and link]. For each product, write a DM reply someone gets when they comment the keyword [keyword]. Under 40 words, warm, in my voice, with the link and one line about who it's for. Also write replies for "how much?", "is it worth it?" and "I can't afford it right now" that are honest and never pushy.

Weekly check: How many people commented the keyword, and how many bought? If they comment but don't buy, rewrite the DM.

AI stories and mini dramas · Story Studio

Story Studio

Plans your mini drama series so every episode ends on a cliffhanger and your characters stay the same.

Claude or ChatGPTChatGPT for picturesGoogle Flow (free daily credits) for talking clips

Set it up

  1. Pick one idea you'd binge: a setting, a person with a secret, and what's at stake.
  2. Run the starter prompt to plan your first 3 episodes and your cast.
  3. Make each character's picture once and reuse it in every shot. That's what keeps faces from drifting.
  4. Post Episode 1, read the comments, and let viewers vote on what happens next.
You are my Story Studio. I want a short vertical AI drama series (each episode 60 to 90 seconds). My idea: [setting, main character, their secret or problem]. Genre: [romance, thriller, faith, comedy, family]. 1. Give me 3 title options and a one-line hook for the series. 2. Write a CHARACTER CARD for each main character (2 to 3 people): age, look, hair, outfit and one personality trait, detailed enough to make the same picture every time. 3. Outline Episodes 1 to 3: a cold open that grabs in 3 seconds, what happens, and a cliffhanger that makes people want Part 2. 4. End with one comment question for viewers to vote on. Use made-up characters only, never a real person or famous character.

Weekly check: Which episode got the most comments? Ask your Story Studio to lean into what viewers voted for.
