Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. You copy one prompt, paste it, and answer questions about your business. If you can send a text, you can do this.

Why do I need Claude Pro?

Your team runs as scheduled tasks inside Claude, which needs a paid plan. The $47 is for the system, the prompts and the community. It's a one-time payment.

Will it send emails or post for me?

Your team gets everything ready: replies, follow-ups, outreach and posts. You reply to your morning brief with what to send, like "send 1 and 3," and only those go out. Nothing goes out without your yes.

What kind of business is this for?

Your business team is built for local service businesses: cleaning, parties and events, beauty, home services, real estate, food, fitness and coaching. Dream Cast is for content: creators, UGC, TikTok Shop, digital products and AI mini series. Your kit opens both.

Can my team read my texts and DMs?

It reads your email and the apps you connect, like your calendar and Square, PayPal or Stripe. It can't read texts or DMs directly. Forward them to your email or paste them in, and Aria writes the reply right away. The installer also sets up an instant text back for missed calls from your own phone.

Do I have to use my own face?

No. Use your AI twin (you), or create an original avatar or AI influencer that isn't a real person. The Scene Library and Story Studio work with either.

Does it make the videos for me?

More than most, and we want to be upfront about how. Your team writes the scripts, scenes and prompts. With Director Mode, Story Studio films the clips for you after you approve the cost, and then puts the episode together. You still listen to each clip and choose your keepers. Director Mode works on a computer with the Chrome browser. Prefer to do it yourself? Every episode also comes with a simple step-by-step checklist.

What if I get stuck?

Post in the SOS HQ community or reach out for personal help.

Can I add more specialists later?

Yes. Business specialists like Echo, Bloom, Confetti, Bid Radar, Harper and Frame are included. Just ask your installer. In Dream Cast, your kit includes one studio helper at a time, and you can switch whenever you like.
