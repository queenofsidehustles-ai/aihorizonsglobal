Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. You copy one prompt, paste it, and answer questions about your business. If you can send a text, you can do this.

Why do I need Claude Pro?

Your team runs as scheduled tasks inside Claude, which needs a paid plan. The $47 is for the system, the prompts and the community. It's a one-time payment.

Will it send emails or post for me?

Your team drafts. You approve. Nothing goes out without your yes.

What kind of business is this for?

Service businesses, coaches, consultants, content creators, UGC creators, TikTok Shop and live sellers, digital product creators, AI mini drama creators and people just starting out. The installer adapts to your business.

Do I have to use my own face?

No. Use your AI twin (you), or create an original avatar or AI influencer that isn't a real person. The Scene Library and Story Studio work with either.

Does it make the videos for me?

More than most, and we want to be upfront about how. Your team writes the scripts, scenes and prompts. With Director Mode (it needs the Claude in Chrome extension), Story Studio films the clips for you in Google Flow after you approve the credit cost; you still listen to each clip and choose your keepers. Prefer to do it yourself? Make the clips in the apps you like. We recommend ChatGPT for pictures and Google Flow (free daily credits) for talking clips. Then drop your clips in a folder and Claude puts the episode together with captions and your end card. CapCut is the free backup. HeyGen, Higgsfield and Kling work too. Every episode comes with a simple step-by-step checklist. Story Studio walks you through each step.

Can it see my TikTok or Instagram stats and DMs?

Not directly. Your team works from your email and what you paste in. If you paste your numbers or comments, it uses them.

What if I get stuck?

Post in the SOS HQ community or reach out for personal help.

Can I add more specialists later?

Yes. More specialists will be available as add-ons inside SOS HQ.
