Using a Pinterest idea? How to save the picture

Pinterest's red Save button does NOT put the picture on your device. Take a screenshot instead:

  1. Tap 1. Find ideas on Pinterest on a scene. Tap a picture you like so it opens big.
  2. Computer (Mac): press Command + Shift + 4, then drag a box around the picture. It lands on your Desktop.
    iPhone: press the side button + volume up together. It goes to your Photos.
  3. Come back here and tap 2. Saved one? Copy the Pinterest version on the same scene.
  4. In ChatGPT, tap + and add her picture or character sheet first, then the screenshot second. Paste and send.

We only use the Pinterest picture for ideas (outfit, pose, place, light). Her face always comes from her picture.

Two pictures at most. Want a certain outfit AND a certain place? Do two steps: first the outfit (save that picture), then use that new picture with the place. Three pictures at once makes her face drift.

● Dream Cast · Step 3

Make it move

ChatGPT makes pictures. A video app makes them move. This page shows you how, step by step.

The easy way

  1. In your Dream Cast chat, under the picture you like, say: Make it move . Add what she does or says if you want, like "she sips her latte and says Saturday mornings are mine."
  2. Save that picture to your phone. Copy the video prompt Dream Cast gives you.
  3. Open your video app (we recommend Google Flow). Start a new clip from your picture, paste the prompt, and make it.
  4. Download the video. Post it with the AI label turned on.

📸 Your twin photo checklist (30 seconds)
  1. Face a window. Daylight on your face, no harsh shadows.
  2. No filters, no beauty mode, no heavy edits.
  3. Hair the way you usually wear it, and the earrings or glasses you always wear.
  4. Phone at eye level, about an arm's length away. Neutral face or a soft smile.
  5. Take 3: straight on, a little to the left, a little to the right.
  6. Then tap the choices below that match your real features. They help the app keep your face, skin tone and hair the same.
