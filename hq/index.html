● Content Studio

Twin Scene Library

Upload your master photo in ChatGPT, tap Copy on a scene, paste, send. Your twin or avatar keeps the exact same face; only the scene changes.

Step by step, on your phone
  1. Open the ChatGPT app. Tap the pencil (top right) for a new chat.
  2. Tap + next to the message box, then Photos, and pick your master photo.
  3. Come back here and tap Copy prompt on a scene. It says Copied ✓.
  4. In ChatGPT, press and hold the message box, then tap Paste.
  5. Change any [bracket] words if you want, then tap send.
  6. Like it? Press and hold the picture and tap Save. Not quite? Type: "Same scene, try again. Keep her face exactly the same."

No master photo yet? Start with the "Start here" scenes. Always label AI images as AI. Your twin must be you; an avatar must be an original made-up person.
