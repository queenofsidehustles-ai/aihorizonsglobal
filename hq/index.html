● Creator Studio · Story Studio

Content Studio

Your team plans and writes your posts and episodes. You tap, paste and post. Here's how it works, plus 300 scene ideas whenever you want extra inspiration.

How your content gets made: 4 moves
  1. Find your path. Not sure what to sell yet? Your Team Installer asks 6 quick questions and picks one path for your first 30 days: TikTok Shop, a digital product, AI content for businesses, your own service, or a story channel.
  2. Build your cast (one time, about 20 minutes). Ask Creator Studio or Story Studio: "Let's build my cast." You get your character's face, outfits, places and style, and the pictures to make, in order. You make them in ChatGPT and save them in your Cast home (a ChatGPT project). That keeps every face, outfit and room the same.
  3. Get your post cards. Ask: "Plan my posts this week." Or start your own way: "my idea…", share a Pinterest picture, or paste a video that's doing well and say "copy this format." Each card has the hook, script, caption, hashtags, best time to post and the shots. For a mini series, ask Story Studio for the next episode.
  4. Film and post. In your Cast home, attach your character and place pictures and paste each shot. Make the first shot, then ask for the other angles from it. Turn pictures into video in your video app, or let Director Mode film your episodes. Post with the caption on your card, and turn on the AI label.

Picture looks wrong? Attach your character's photo again, or start a new chat in your Cast home and attach the pictures. Faces stay the same when the photo is in the chat.

Using a scene idea yourself, step by step
  1. Open your Cast home in ChatGPT (or the picture or video app you like: Google Flow, Gemini, HeyGen, Higgsfield and Kling work too).
  2. Upload your master photo (your twin or your avatar). For product scenes, upload the real product photo too.
  3. Come back here and tap Copy prompt on a scene. It says Copied ✓.
  4. Paste it into your app, change any [bracket] words, and send.
  5. Like it? Save it. Not quite? Type: "Same scene, try again. Keep the face exactly the same."
  6. Want a look you saw on Pinterest? Tap Ideas on Pinterest, save a picture you love, then use the "Use my Pinterest pick" scene in Start here.

No master photo yet? Start with the "Start here" scenes: one set for your twin (you), one for an avatar (an original made-up person). Always label AI images and videos as AI. Never use a real person's face, and never present AI as a real customer review.

📸 Your twin photo checklist (30 seconds)
  1. Face a window. Daylight on your face, no harsh shadows.
  2. No filters, no beauty mode, no heavy edits.
  3. Hair the way you usually wear it, and the earrings or glasses you always wear.
  4. Phone at eye level, about an arm's length away. Neutral face or a soft smile.
  5. Take 3: straight on, a little to the left, a little to the right.
  6. Then tap the choices below that match your real features. They help the app keep your face, skin tone and hair the same.
