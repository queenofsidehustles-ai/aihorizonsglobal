Dream Cast

Your AI character, the same face in every picture.

Dream Cast is a friendly picture helper inside ChatGPT for women building content with an AI character: a twin of themselves or an avatar they made up. Attach your character's picture, tell Dream Cast what you want in plain words, and it keeps her face, skin tone, hair and body the same every time. No tech skills needed.

What it does

🎬 Character sheet
One master sheet: 4 full-body views, 2 face close-ups and a list of her features.
📸 New scenes
Put her in a kitchen, a café, a podcast studio, anywhere you describe.
👗 Outfit swap
Attach an outfit picture and she wears it, same face, same body.
👯 Two characters
Put two of your characters in one picture for mini-dramas.
🎞️ Video planning
A scene-by-scene storyboard with ready-to-paste video prompts.
🔧 Quick fixes
Face changed? Skin too smooth? Say so and it fixes it in the same chat.

How to use it

  1. Open Dream Cast in ChatGPT.
  2. Say hello. Dream Cast shows you a menu.
  3. Pick what you want, attach your picture, and send.

Good to know

  • Pictures are made by ChatGPT's own image tool, under your ChatGPT plan.
  • Dream Cast never makes pictures of real people (except your own photo), celebrities or realistic children.
  • Turn on the AI label when you post AI pictures on social media.
