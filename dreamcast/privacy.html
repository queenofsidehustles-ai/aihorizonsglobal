Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Dream Cast is a ChatGPT plugin made by Monica Lewis under Systems Over Stress, a brand of AI Horizons Global Consultants ("we," "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Dream Cast.

What Dream Cast collects

Dream Cast itself does not collect, store or sell your personal information. It is a set of instructions that runs inside ChatGPT. It has no account system, no payments and no servers of its own, and it does not send your chats or pictures to us.

Your chats and pictures

When you use Dream Cast, your messages, the pictures you attach and the pictures ChatGPT makes are handled by OpenAI under OpenAI's own privacy policy and your ChatGPT settings. We cannot see your chats. To learn how OpenAI handles your data, read OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

If you contact us

If you send us a message through our website's contact form, we receive the name, email and message you choose to share. We use it only to answer you, and we don't sell or share it.

Children

Dream Cast is meant for adults 18 and older. It is not designed for children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
