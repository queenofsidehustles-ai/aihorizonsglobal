Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Dream Cast is a ChatGPT plugin made by Monica Lewis under Systems Over Stress, a brand of AI Horizons Global Consultants ("we," "us"). Dream Cast provides instructions for creating character pictures and planning video storyboards in ChatGPT. It does not create its own user accounts, accept payments, or run a separate image-generation service.

ChatGPT messages and pictures

When you use Dream Cast, OpenAI processes your messages, the pictures you attach, and the pictures ChatGPT creates to provide ChatGPT and its image features. OpenAI's Privacy Policy and your ChatGPT settings apply. We do not receive or access your Dream Cast chats or pictures.

Information you send through our website

Our website contact form may ask for your name, email address, organization, and message. The organization field is optional. We use this information to answer your question or provide support. Website hosting, form-handling, and email providers may process it as needed to run the form and deliver messages. We do not sell this information or use it for unrelated marketing.

We keep support messages for up to 45 days after our last reply, then delete them from our active support records. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you sent through the form by contacting us through the Dream Cast support page.

Website technical information

Our website host may process basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser or device details, to deliver and protect the website. The host's privacy practices apply to its handling of that information.

Children and intended audience

Dream Cast is designed for adult creators, and its content is suitable for general audiences, including users ages 13–17. It is not intended for children under 13 or for mature or adult content.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions

For questions or requests about information you sent through our website, contact us through the Dream Cast support page. For ChatGPT messages and pictures, use OpenAI's Privacy Policy and the controls in your ChatGPT account. We cannot access or delete that content for you.
